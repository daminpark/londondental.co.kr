Fiona grew up bilingual, trained at two of the best dental schools in the world, and is one of very few dentists in Seoul licensed to practice in both the UK and Korea.

She opened London Dental Clinic because she wanted to do things differently — no rushing, no overtreatment, and genuine communication with every patient.

---

- **BDS** — Queen Mary University of London, Barts and The London School of Medicine and Dentistry (2005–2010)
- **MSD, Dental Anesthesia** — Seoul National University, School of Dentistry (2013–)
- **Doctoral Course, Preventive Dentistry** — Seoul National University (ongoing)
