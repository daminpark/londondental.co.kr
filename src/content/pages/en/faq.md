#### Is there an English-speaking dentist in Seoul?

Yes! London Dental Clinic is run by Dr. Fiona Park, who is fully fluent in both English and Korean. She trained at Barts and The London School of Medicine and Dentistry (Queen Mary University of London) and Seoul National University. She holds dental licenses in both the UK and South Korea, making her uniquely qualified to serve international patients in Seoul.

#### Do you accept Korean health insurance?

Yes, we accept Korean National Health Insurance for covered procedures (scaling, some fillings, extractions, senior implants/dentures, etc.). Please inquire about specific treatments.

#### Can I use my international insurance?

We provide detailed English receipts and documentation for you to claim reimbursement from your international provider. Direct billing is typically not possible, but most international insurance providers accept our documentation for reimbursement.

#### How much does dental care cost in Seoul?

Dental care in Seoul is significantly more affordable than in the US, UK, or Australia. For example, dental implants and cosmetic procedures cost a fraction of Western prices while maintaining the same quality standards. We provide transparent pricing and will discuss all costs before beginning treatment.

#### Where is London Dental Clinic located?

We are located at 97 Jong-ro 5-gil, 2nd Floor, Jongno-gu, Seoul (near Gwanghwamun). The clinic is a 5-minute walk from Gwanghwamun Station (Line 5), Jonggak Station (Line 1), and Gyeongbokgung Station (Line 3). We are close to Gyeongbok Palace, Gwanghwamun Square, and Cheonggyecheon Stream.

#### How far in advance should I book?

Booking a few days to a week in advance is recommended, especially for specific times. Urgent cases will be accommodated as quickly as possible. You can book via Naver Booking or call us directly at +82-2-732-1917.

#### What dental services do you offer?

We offer a comprehensive range of dental services including cosmetic dentistry (whitening, veneers), dental implants, crowns and bridges, dentures, fillings, root canal treatment, wisdom tooth extraction, oral surgery, checkups, and professional teeth cleaning (scaling). All treatments are explained in English.

#### Can tourists or short-term visitors get dental treatment?

Absolutely. We regularly treat tourists, business travelers, and short-term visitors. No Korean residency is required. We can often accommodate same-day or next-day appointments for visitors with limited time in Seoul.

#### Is parking available?

Paid parking is available in nearby buildings. Please contact us for recommendations as we do not have dedicated patient parking. The clinic is most conveniently reached by subway.
