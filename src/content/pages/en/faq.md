#### Do you accept Korean health insurance?

Yes, we accept Korean National Health Insurance for covered procedures (scaling, some fillings, extractions, senior implants/dentures, etc.). Please inquire about specific treatments.

#### Can I use my international insurance?

We provide detailed English receipts and documentation for you to claim reimbursement from your international provider. Direct billing is typically not possible.

#### How far in advance should I book?

Booking a few days to a week in advance is recommended, especially for specific times. Urgent cases will be accommodated as quickly as possible.

#### Is parking available?

Paid parking is available in nearby buildings. Please contact us for recommendations as we do not have dedicated patient parking.
