Welcome to London Dental Clinic! We want your first visit to be comfortable and informative. Here's what you can typically expect:

#### 1. Welcome & Consultation

We'll begin with a friendly chat to understand your dental history, concerns, and goals. This is a great time to ask any initial questions.

#### 2. Comprehensive Examination

Dr. Park will conduct a thorough examination of your teeth, gums, jaw, and surrounding tissues. This includes a visual inspection and may involve checking your bite and screening for oral cancer.

#### 3. Digital Diagnostics

If necessary, we'll take digital X-rays (like bitewings or potentially a CT scan if needed for specific issues). These low-radiation images help us see between teeth and below the gum line.

#### 4. Treatment Planning

Based on the exam and diagnostics, Dr. Park will discuss her findings with you in clear terms (English or Korean). We'll explain any recommended treatments, alternatives, timelines, and associated costs, creating a personalized plan together.

#### 5. Cleaning (If Scheduled)

Often, your first visit may include a professional cleaning (scaling) by our dental hygienist to remove plaque and tartar build-up.

#### 6. Next Steps & Home Care

We'll finalize your treatment plan, schedule any necessary follow-up appointments, and provide personalized advice on maintaining your oral health at home.

Our goal is to ensure you leave fully informed and comfortable with your path to a healthier smile.
