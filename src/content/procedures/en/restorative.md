#### Bonding & Fillings

**Bonding** uses tooth-colored composite resin to repair chips, cracks, gaps, or misshapen teeth. It's one of the simplest and most affordable cosmetic procedures — usually completed in a single visit.

**Fillings** replace decayed or damaged tooth material. We offer:
- **Composite resin** — tooth-colored, natural appearance
- **Dental amalgam** — traditional, highly durable

Dr. Park will recommend the best material based on the location, extent of damage, and your preference.

#### Root Canal Treatment

Root canal treatment is needed when decay or trauma reaches the **pulp** (the living tissue inside the tooth). Left untreated, infection can spread to the surrounding bone.

**Signs you may need a root canal:**
- Sensitivity to hot, cold, or sweets
- Pain when biting or applying pressure
- Swelling around the tooth
- Persistent bad taste

**The procedure:** The infected pulp is removed, canals are cleaned and disinfected, then filled to prevent reinfection. A crown is usually recommended afterward to protect the treated tooth.

#### Non-Surgical Gum Treatment

Unhealthy gums compromise the foundation of your teeth. **Scaling and root planing** (deep cleaning) removes plaque and tartar from below the gumline, allowing tissue to heal.

**Warning signs:**
- Gums that bleed easily or appear red
- Persistent bad breath
- Gums pulling away from teeth
- Loose teeth or bite changes

Combined with improved daily hygiene, non-surgical treatment is often sufficient to control gum disease.
