When a tooth can't be saved, safe and careful extraction is the priority. Dr. Park's training in oral and maxillofacial surgery ensures precision in every case.

---

#### When extraction is needed

- Severe decay beyond repair
- Advanced gum disease
- Infection or abscess
- Fractured teeth or roots
- Impacted or malpositioned teeth
- Orthodontic preparation

Before extraction, Dr. Park takes X-rays to assess the tooth position and surrounding bone. Aftercare instructions, pain management, and follow-up are all provided.

#### Wisdom Teeth

Wisdom teeth (third molars) often lack space to emerge properly. **Impacted wisdom teeth** — partially or fully trapped in gum or bone — can cause:

- Infection and swelling
- Damage to neighboring teeth
- Cyst formation

Dr. Park may recommend extraction even before symptoms appear to prevent future complications.

#### Minor Oral Surgery

Includes removal of retained roots, broken teeth, and small cysts or lesions. All procedures are performed under **local anesthesia** and are routine at our clinic.
