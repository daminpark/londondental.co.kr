Implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth — they look, feel, and function like natural teeth.

---

#### What is a dental implant?

A dental implant is a small **titanium post** surgically placed into your jawbone. It acts as an artificial tooth root, providing a stable foundation for a crown, bridge, or denture.

**Key benefits:**
- Looks and feels like a natural tooth
- Preserves jawbone (prevents bone loss)
- Doesn't affect neighboring teeth
- Can last a lifetime with proper care

#### The process

1. **Consultation & planning** — Dr. Park evaluates your bone density and overall oral health using digital imaging
2. **Implant placement** — the titanium post is placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia
3. **Healing period** — 3–6 months for the implant to fuse with the bone (osseointegration)
4. **Crown placement** — a custom-made crown is attached to the implant

#### Insurance coverage

Patients aged **65 and over** may be eligible for Korean National Health Insurance coverage for implants. Dr. Park can advise on your specific eligibility during consultation.
