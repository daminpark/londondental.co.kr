---

#### Crowns

A crown completely covers a damaged tooth, restoring its strength, shape, and appearance. Crowns are also placed on implants to create a tooth-like structure.

**Common uses:**
- Protecting a weakened or cracked tooth
- Restoring a severely worn or decayed tooth
- Covering a dental implant
- Improving the appearance of a discolored tooth

#### Bridges

Bridges replace one or more missing teeth by anchoring to the natural teeth or implants on either side of the gap.

- **Abutment teeth** — the anchoring teeth are fitted with crowns
- **Pontic** — the replacement tooth is attached between them
- Multiple material options available based on location, function, and aesthetics

#### Dentures

Removable replacements for missing teeth. Two types available:

- **Complete dentures** — when all teeth are missing
- **Partial dentures** — when some natural teeth remain (also prevents remaining teeth from shifting)

**Insurance note:** If you are **65 or older** with Korean health insurance, you are covered for partial and/or complete dentures (upper and lower jaw) every seven years.
