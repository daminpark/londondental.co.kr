#### Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic procedures correct imperfections and enhance your smile's appearance. Options include:

- **Veneers** — thin porcelain shells bonded to front teeth
- **Bonding** — tooth-colored resin to repair chips or gaps
- **Crowns** — full coverage for damaged or discolored teeth
- **Reshaping** — subtle contouring for a more balanced smile

Whether you have stained, chipped, misshapen, or gapped teeth, there's a treatment to match your needs and budget.

#### Teeth Whitening

We use **custom tray whitening** — a gradual, effective method that produces long-lasting results.

**How it works:**
1. We create custom-fitted trays from impressions of your teeth
2. You apply professional whitening gel at home
3. Wear for a few hours daily or overnight
4. Results typically visible within several days

The trays are yours to keep for future touch-ups. This method is safer and more controlled than one-size-fits-all alternatives.
