# Public Ops Playbook Example

This redacted note replaces an internal Korean setup guide that previously lived in the repository. The original guide included account-specific and client-facing instructions, so it should not be published in a portfolio repo.

The useful public pattern is kept here: a small-business website launch needs both code and operational follow-through.

## Launch Checklist Pattern

- Confirm the production domain and canonical URLs.
- Submit the sitemap to search consoles after deployment.
- Verify ownership using the official search-console method chosen by the site owner.
- Keep business profile information consistent across Google, Naver, maps, and the website.
- Ask the business owner to confirm service categories, hours, accessibility details, and booking links before publishing.
- Request reviews only in ways that comply with platform policies and do not offer incentives.
- Keep account credentials, verification tokens, private emails, and owner-only instructions outside the public repository.

## What Belongs In Public

- General process notes.
- Non-sensitive architecture decisions.
- Public URLs that are already visible on the website.
- Redacted examples that show operational thinking without exposing private workflow.

## What Stays Private

- Login accounts and account ownership notes.
- Search-console verification tokens before publication.
- Internal client instructions.
- Review collection scripts or messages that have not been approved by the business owner.
- Any patient or appointment information.
