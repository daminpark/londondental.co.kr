# Demo Checklist

Use this checklist before pinning or sharing the repo.

## Desktop

- Home page loads with doctor hero image and primary booking/call actions.
- Header language switch goes between `/en/` and `/ko/`.
- Procedure cards open as modal content and direct routes still work.
- Gallery lightbox opens and closes.
- Location map and Naver/Google map links are visible.

## Mobile

- Mobile hero image and text fit without overlap.
- Sticky booking bar appears after scrolling.
- Mobile menu opens, closes, and preserves language switch.
- Procedure modal behaves as a bottom sheet.
- Reviews carousel scrolls horizontally.

## Routes

- `/en/`
- `/ko/`
- `/en/services`
- `/ko/services`
- `/en/faq`
- `/ko/faq`
- `/en/privacy-policy`
- `/ko/privacy-policy`
- `/llms.txt`
- `/llms-full.txt`
- `/llms-ko.txt`

## Public Readiness

- Hours, ratings, review counts, and booking links are owner-verified.
- Clinic assets and review excerpts have permission for public use.
- No private setup notes, account instructions, credentials, or patient information are committed.
